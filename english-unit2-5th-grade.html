מה התרגום הנכון?

---

The School Race

Today is the big race at school. Many children, men, and women are there. It is very important to practice every day to be a winner. My friend Avi is in the race. He wants to win a gold medal and a prize. There are many parents and teachers watching. They are very excited and they make a lot of noise. Avi has a problem with his legs, but he moves fast. There are five birds in the trees and lots of cars on the road near the school. It is a special day!
